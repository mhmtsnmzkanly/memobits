# Memobits language feature inventory

Baseline: `113ec09`. Its only changes since `863a45e` are two unlinked Rust preparation modules. The latest shipped language slice is the direct internal read-only borrow of a String-owning struct. This inventory is the catalog of language intent and implementation status. Use the [local implementation plan](../history/local-implementation-handoff.md) for sequencing and the [tranche requirements](launch-manifest.md) for implementation contracts. Inclusion here does not authorize a feature or settle its spelling. Statuses were refreshed on 2026-11-23 against `implementation-requirements.md` and `launch-progress.md`; where they disagree, those two files win.

For implementation, the [canonical launch prompt](../history/local-implementation-handoff.md#canonical-launch-prompt) and [full launch manifest](launch-manifest.md#full-launch-manifest) select the required work. The catalog below includes historical and future ideas outside that scope. The delegated defaults and blocker policy apply to this launch. They do not retroactively turn every candidate into an approved language feature.

## Reading the inventory

- **Shipped** means there is source-to-execution support within the stated limits. Syntax or an IR representation alone does not qualify.
- **Partial** means there is a concrete implemented subset and a known remaining boundary.
- **Accepted** means the language direction is settled. Unshipped accepted work may still have open spellings or implementation decisions.
- **Planned** means a roadmap or handoff selects implementation work. It carries no additional language decision.
- **Open** marks a decision or candidate, including historical ideas without adoption. **Deferred** marks deliberately postponed scope. **Rejected** identifies a behavior excluded by the current contract. **Superseded** marks an earlier design displaced by a later rule. Rejected forms require a new decision to reconsider.
- For behavior at this baseline, trust code, reachable executable examples and tests, then current implementation checkpoints. For intent, start with the [accepted decisions](decisions.md), then documented clarifications that do not conflict with them. The [tracked implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) gives the broad roadmap. The [design-only guide](proposed-language-guide.mb), dated audits and local notebooks contain examples, proposals and superseded rules. They do not extend the executable subset. Tests described below are acceptance criteria or existing evidence, as indicated. The inventory was prepared by inspection.
